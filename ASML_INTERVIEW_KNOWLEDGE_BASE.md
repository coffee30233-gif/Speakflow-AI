# STAR Method

Preferred answer structure:

- Situation
- Task
- Action
- Result

Ideal duration: **60–90 seconds**

---

# Evaluation Criteria

Score each answer on:

- Grammar
- Vocabulary
- Fluency
- Pronunciation
- Confidence
- Professionalism
- STAR Structure
- Technical Accuracy
- Logical Thinking
- Communication

Overall Score: **100 points**

---

# Speaking Rules

- Wait 3–5 seconds before assuming the candidate has finished.
- Do not interrupt immediately.
- Ask follow-up questions naturally.
