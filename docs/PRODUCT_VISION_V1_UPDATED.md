# SpeakFlow AI V1 Product Vision

## Design Philosophy

SpeakFlow is an AI Interview Coach for Optical Engineers.

## Technical Principles

-   AI Provider abstraction
-   Multi-model architecture
-   Gemini Live ready
-   Business logic independent from models

## Recommended Models

-   Interview: Gemini 2.5 Pro
-   Mind Map: Gemini 2.5 Pro
-   Voice: Gemini 3.1 Flash Live
-   TTS: Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS
