# Tell Me About Yourself

Hello, my name is Lynn.

I have a master's degree in Applied Physics from National Dong Hwa University.

I have approximately six years of experience as a Senior Optical Engineer.

My work mainly involves optical system measurement, optical alignment, troubleshooting, performance verification, root cause analysis, and production support.

I have hands-on experience with Beam Profilers, Fizeau Interferometers, Collimators, laser systems, optical filters, and image quality evaluation.

Recently, I have become increasingly interested in manufacturing excellence and solving complex engineering problems, which is why I would like to join ASML.

---

# Why ASML

ASML is the global leader in semiconductor lithography technology.

I admire its innovation, international working environment, and engineering culture.

I believe my optics background and troubleshooting experience can contribute to production engineering while allowing me to continue learning and growing.
